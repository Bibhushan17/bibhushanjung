About

The Designer
Behind the Work

Hi, I'm Bibhushan Jung Sijapati — a graphic designer from Kathmandu with over six years of experience creating visual work that communicates, converts, and captivates.

I specialise in social media design, product visuals, poster design and brand campaigns. I've worked across agencies, freelance, and education — currently teaching design at two colleges while continuing to grow as a practitioner.

"Design is not just what it looks like — it's how it works, how it feels, and how it moves people."
